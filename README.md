## About Me

I am a Data Science student at the University of Lagos and a self-taught software engineer with a bias for building things that actually work.

I co-founded **TeenovateX Labs** — a digital-first innovation hub empowering teenagers across Africa with tech exposure, mentorship, and community. I am also active in **Setld**, running growth and marketing for a product I believe in.

This year I am deep in the entrepreneurship journey again. Building. Collaborating. Running an NGO. Implementing everything I have absorbed from reading and watching people who think sharply about the world. Let's see where this goes.

My signature is simple: *systems over motivation, leverage over hustle, execution over ideas.*
